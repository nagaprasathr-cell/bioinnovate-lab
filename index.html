Featured Experiment

Estimation of Carbohydrates in Packaged Biscuits Using the Anthrone Method

Aim

To estimate carbohydrate content in a packaged biscuit sample using a validated Anthrone method.

Principle

Under strongly acidic conditions, carbohydrates form products that react with Anthrone to produce a coloured solution. Absorbance is measured using a spectrophotometer under the conditions specified by the validated method.

Materials

Biscuit sample, distilled water, suitable test tubes, Anthrone reagent, spectrophotometer, water bath, glucose standards, and appropriate laboratory PPE.

Procedure Overview

  1. Crush 2 g of biscuit and dissolve it in 100 mL of distilled water.
  2. Transfer 2 mL of the prepared sample into a suitable test tube.
  3. Reagent addition, including concentrated sulfuric acid, must follow a validated protocol under qualified laboratory supervision.
  4. Use the specified water-bath conditions and cooling procedure in the approved method.
  5. Measure absorbance at the wavelength specified by the validated method.
  6. Compare the sample with glucose standards and use a standard curve to estimate concentration.
Safety notice

Anthrone reagent and concentrated sulfuric acid are hazardous. This experiment must be conducted by trained personnel with suitable PPE, appropriate ventilation, approved handling procedures, and proper chemical-waste disposal. Never handle these reagents without supervision.

Observation and Calculation

Record the actual absorbance readings and the glucose standard concentrations used. Calculate sample concentration from the standard curve and apply the appropriate dilution factors. No experimental results are provided because measurements have not been performed.
